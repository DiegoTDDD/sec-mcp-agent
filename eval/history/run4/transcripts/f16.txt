Q: What does Chevron say about the volatility of crude oil prices?

Chevron Corp (CVX) states in its 10-K filing that it is primarily in a commodities business that has a history of price volatility. The most significant factor affecting the company’s results of operations is the prices of crude oil, natural gas, and natural gas liquids, which can be influenced by various factors including general economic conditions, industry production and inventory levels, technology advancements, geopolitical risks, and governmental regulations. The company evaluates the risk of changing commodity prices as a core part of its business planning process. An investment in the company carries significant exposure to fluctuations in global prices of crude oil, natural gas, and natural gas liquids.
