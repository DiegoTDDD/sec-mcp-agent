Q: How does Coca-Cola describe its operating segments?

Coca-Cola Co (KO) describes its operating segments primarily based on geographic responsibility, which is consistent with the way management runs its business. The operating segments are generally subdivided into smaller geographic regions, which are reporting units. The Bottling Investments operating segment includes all of its consolidated bottling operations, regardless of geographic location, and each consolidated bottling operation within the Bottling Investments operating segment is its own reporting unit. Goodwill is assigned to the reporting unit or units that benefit from the synergies arising from each business combination.
