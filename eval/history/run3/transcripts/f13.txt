Q: What does UnitedHealth Group say about medical cost trends?

UnitedHealth Group Inc. (UNH) discusses medical cost trends in their 10-K filings. The trends primarily relate to changes in unit costs, care activity, and prescription drug costs. They have observed increased care patterns, higher intensity of services, and increased health care unit costs. The Inflation Reduction Act (IRA) has also altered the Medicare Part D model and benefits, shifting more risk to plans, which results in increased premiums and medical costs. Additionally, the member profile of newly added patients under value-based care arrangements, additional people served by their Medicare Advantage plans in markets where other plans exited, and people served within their individual exchange business have contributed to increased medical costs. These trends may continue in future periods.
