Q: What does NVIDIA report about US export controls and their effect on its sales?

NVIDIA reports that US export controls have and are likely to have a disproportionate impact on NVIDIA and may disadvantage the company against certain of its competitors that sell chips outside the scope of such control. Export controls have already and may in the future encourage customers outside China and other impacted regions to 'design-out' certain U.S. semiconductors from their products to reduce the compliance burden and risk, and to ensure that they are able to serve markets worldwide. Export controls have already encouraged and may in the future encourage overseas governments to request that our customers purchase from our competitors rather than NVIDIA or other U.S. firms, harming the business, market position, and financial results.
