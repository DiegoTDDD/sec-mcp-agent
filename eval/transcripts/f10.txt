Q: What does Boeing say about 737 production and quality?

Boeing Co (BA) in its 10-K filing mentions that in the U.S., their commercial aircraft products are required to comply with FAA regulations governing production and quality systems, airworthiness and installation approvals, repair procedures and continuing operational safety. Additionally, the 737-9 door plug accident in January 2024 led to an investigation by the FAA into the 737 quality control system, including additional requirements and restrictions. Boeing also slowed production rates and delayed planned production rate increases to address these issues, impacting their financial position, results of operations, and cash flows during 2024 and 2025.
