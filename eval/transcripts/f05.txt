Q: How could climate change regulation affect Exxon Mobil's business, according to its 10-K?

Exxon Mobil's pursuit of lower-emission and other new business opportunities, such as carbon capture and storage, hydrogen and ammonia, lower-emission fuels, ProxximaTM resin systems, carbon materials, low-carbon data centers, and lithium, depends on the growth and development of markets for these products and services, including supportive and stable government policies and developments in existing and new technology. Additionally, the company faces security concerns, such as civil unrest, military conflict, and cybersecurity attacks, which could disrupt operations. Furthermore, the company notes that international accords and national regulations related to greenhouse gas emissions are evolving with uncertain timing and outcome, making it difficult to predict their business impact. The company also acknowledges that people and nations will continue to need practical solutions for reducing risks of global climate change without jeopardizing the affordability or reliability of energy.
