Q: What risks does Microsoft describe related to artificial intelligence?

Microsoft Corp describes risks related to artificial intelligence in their filings, including the potential for adversaries to use vulnerabilities to install malware and the risk of inadequate account security practices leading to unauthorized access. Additionally, they mention the risk of cyberattacks and security vulnerabilities leading to reduced revenue, increased costs, liability claims, or harm to their reputation or competitive position. They also highlight the complexity of their products and the potential for defects in design, manufacture, or associated software to adversely affect their business and results of operations.
