Q: How does Coca-Cola describe its operating segments?

Coca-Cola Co (KO) describes its operating segments based on geographic responsibility, which is consistent with the way management runs the business. The company's geographic operating segments are generally subdivided into smaller geographic regions, which are reporting units. The operating segments include North America, EMEA (Eurasia and Middle East), Latin America, Asia Pacific, and Bottling Investments. Each consolidated bottling operation within the Bottling Investments operating segment is its own reporting unit.
