Q: What does Visa say about interchange fee regulation or litigation?

Visa Inc. (V) mentions in its 10-K filing that the EU's Interchange Fee Regulation caps consumer credit and debit interchange rates for transactions within the European Economic Area (EEA). The European Commission (EC) may periodically reassess these regulations, which could lead to further reductions or expansion of regulation to additional products, services, and fees. Additionally, Visa Inc. (V) notes in its 10-K filing that the U.S. Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform and Consumer Act limits interchange reimbursement rates for certain debit card transactions in the U.S., and the European Union (EU) Interchange Fee Regulation limits interchange rates in the EEA. Visa also mentions that the Reserve Bank of Australia regulates permissible levels of interchange for both domestic and cross-border transactions.
