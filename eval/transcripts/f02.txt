Q: What does NVIDIA report about US export controls and their effect on its sales?

NVIDIA reports that US export controls have and are likely to have a disproportionate impact on NVIDIA and may disadvantage the company against certain of its competitors that sell chips outside the scope of such control. These controls have already encouraged customers outside China and other impacted regions to 'design-out' certain U.S. semiconductors from their products to reduce the compliance burden and risk, and to ensure they are able to serve markets worldwide. Export controls have also encouraged and may encourage overseas governments to request that their customers purchase from NVIDIA's competitors rather than NVIDIA, harming the company's business, market position, and financial results. Additionally, export controls have negatively impacted demand for NVIDIA's products and services, leading to excess inventory or purchase obligations, and have increased the risk of investing in U.S. advanced semiconductor products.
