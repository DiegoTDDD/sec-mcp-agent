Q: How does JPMorgan Chase describe its cybersecurity risk?

JPMorgan Chase & Co. describes its cybersecurity risk as evolving techniques used in cyber attacks, the possibility of third-party access to internal networks, and the potential for disruption or damage to its systems and those of its clients, customers, and counterparties. The company also faces risks such as misappropriation of confidential information, financial loss, and reputational harm. Additionally, the company faces risks related to liquidity and capital, and the integration of acquired businesses, including system integration and the strengthening of network security.
